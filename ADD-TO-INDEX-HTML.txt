ONE-LINE CHANGE
===============
Open index.html on GitHub (pencil icon). Find this line near the top:

<meta name="theme-color" content="#0b0d12">

Paste this ONE line directly under it:

<script src="/pwa-install.js" defer></script>

Then upload these files to the SAME folder as index.html:
  - pwa-install.js
  - manifest.json
  - sw.js
  - icons/ (the whole folder: icon-192.png, icon-512.png, maskable-512.png)

Commit. After your site redeploys, open it on your phone and tap Install app
(iPhone: Share -> Add to Home Screen). For an APK use pwabuilder.com with your live URL.
